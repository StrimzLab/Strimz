<Callout type="warn" title="Coming, not live">
  Cross-chain payments are coming. Today customers pay on Arc only, and checkout has no source-chain
  picker. Keep your customers on Arc for now.
</Callout>

Strimz settles on Arc, where USDC is the native gas token. Some customers
hold their USDC on another chain. The plan is to let them pay from that
chain with Circle's [CCTP V2](https://developers.circle.com/stablecoins/cctp-getting-started),
so they don't have to bridge first.

## What exists today

Only part of the destination side is built. The agent has a bridge worker
that polls Circle's attestation API for a burn, every 30 seconds for up to
120 polls (about an hour). Nothing in checkout starts a bridge yet, so the
worker never receives a job in production. When attestation doesn't land
in time, the worker gives up and logs it. There is no automatic refund.

## What's coming

1. The customer picks their source chain in checkout.
2. They sign a `depositForBurn` on that chain, which burns USDC.
3. Strimz waits for Circle's attestation.
4. The scheduler calls `MessageTransmitter.receiveMessage(message, attestation)`
   on Arc and the USDC lands in your wallet.

This page will describe the supported chains, dashboard view and failure
handling once the feature ships.
