Refunds send stablecoin back to the wallet that paid. They are on-chain: your own Strimz wallet signs and sends the transfer, and the money leaves your payout address when it confirms.

## The layout

The Refunds table lists every refund your account has created, most recent first. Columns:

- **Refund**: the refund id
- **Original tx**: the transaction being refunded
- **Amount**: how much you're returning, in the transaction's currency. Full or partial.
- **Reason**
- **Status**
- **To wallet**: the payer's wallet, which receives the refund
- **Created**

## Creating a refund

The easiest way is from the payment itself:

1. Open **Transactions**, then the payment you want to refund.
2. Click **Refund**. The dialog opens with the transaction filled in and the amount in its currency (USDC or EURC).
3. Enter the amount, pick a reason, and add an optional note.
4. Click **Create + sign**. Your Strimz wallet asks you to sign the transfer.

You can also click **New refund** on this page and paste a transaction ID. The dialog looks the transaction up and shows its amount and currency before you continue.

The amount must be above 0 and no more than the original payment. Across several partial refunds, the total cannot exceed the original payment. A refund that is still waiting for your signature counts toward that total.

## Statuses

**Awaiting signature.** The refund is created and waits for your wallet to sign the transfer. If you closed the wallet prompt, use **Sign & submit** in the row menu to try again.

**Submitted.** Your wallet sent the transfer, and Strimz is waiting to see it on-chain.

**Completed.** The transfer confirmed. The `refund.completed` webhook fires, and the payer gets a receipt email if they left an email at checkout.

The API also defines `pending`, `failed` and `cancelled`. The dashboard shows them if a refund ever has one.

## Common workflows

**A customer wants their money back.** Transactions → open the payment → **Refund** → sign. Two minutes end to end.

**A customer overpaid.** Refund part of the payment. The dialog accepts any amount up to the original.

**I want to send stablecoin that isn't tied to a payment.** Refunds always need a source payment. Use the Withdraw page or your own wallet instead.
