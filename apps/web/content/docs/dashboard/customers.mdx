title: Customers page
description: Every wallet that's paid you, and a page per customer with everything they've done.
---

Customers lists every payer wallet that has interacted with your account. A "customer" in Strimz is the pair `(merchantId, walletAddress)`. If the same wallet pays two different merchants, each merchant sees their own separate customer record.

## The list

One row per customer. Columns:

- **Customer**: the display name if you set one through the API, otherwise their email. The email shows underneath.
- **Wallet**: a short form of the payer's address.
- **External ref**: your own ID for this customer, if you set one through the API.
- **First seen** and **Last activity**: relative time.

Click the name or the wallet, or choose **View customer** in the row's menu, to open the customer page.

The search box matches name, email and wallet. **Export CSV** downloads the customers you've loaded, with ID, wallet, email, display name, external ref and dates.

## The customer page

The top of the page shows:

- the full wallet address;
- the email receipts go to;
- your external ref;
- when you first saw them and when they were last active.

Copy buttons sit next to each value.

Below that, one section each for their **payments** (payment links they opened), **subscriptions**, **transactions**, **refunds** and **invoices**. Each section shows the newest 25, and each row opens its own page. A refund opens the payment it refunded.

The page doesn't add up a total spend. USDC and EURC are different currencies and Strimz never adds them together.

## Emails

The email comes from the payer at checkout, or from you when you create the customer through the API. It never replaces the wallet as their identity.

Strimz keeps the **first** email a wallet gives you. If the same wallet types a different email at a later checkout, the stored email doesn't change. Receipts always go to the stored email, the one shown as "Receipts go to" on the customer page.

## Common workflows

**A customer says "I paid but didn't get a receipt".** Search the list for their wallet, or call `GET /v1/customers?walletAddress=0x…`. Open the customer page and check "Receipts go to". If that address has a typo, that's why the mail didn't arrive.

**I want to build a mailing list from paying customers.** Use **Export CSV** on the Customers page and keep the rows that have an email.

**I want to see everything one customer has done.** Open their customer page. Through the API, pass `customerId` to the payment sessions, subscriptions, transactions, refunds or invoices list.

## What's _not_ on this page

- **No account creation for customers.** Strimz doesn't require payers to sign up. If you need customer accounts, you build that layer on your side using the wallet as the primary key.
- **No PII beyond email.** Strimz does not collect names, addresses, or phone numbers at checkout. Everything you see here comes from what the payer entered or what you set through the API.
