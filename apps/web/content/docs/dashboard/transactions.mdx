Transactions are what actually happened on-chain. A payment session, an invoice, a storefront purchase or a subscription charge becomes a transaction once its payment confirms. Refunds appear here too.

## The layout

The table lists transactions in this mode, most recent first. Columns:

- **ID**: opens the transaction's page
- **Kind**: Payment, Subscription charge, Invoice payment, Storefront purchase or Refund
- **Amount** and **Net**: in the transaction's own currency. Net is what reached your payout address after the Strimz fee.
- **Payer**: the wallet that paid
- **Status**: `confirmed`, `pending` or `failed`
- **When**: the block time

Filter by kind, status and date above the table. For dates, pick **Last 7 days**, **This month** or **Last month**, or choose **Custom** and set a first and last day. Both days are included, and days follow your computer's time zone.

**Export CSV** downloads the rows you've loaded, with amounts in base units (6 decimals). When a date range is set, the file is named after it, for example `transactions-2026-09-01-to-2026-09-30.csv`.

## A transaction's page

Each transaction shows:

- **Amounts:** the amount, the Strimz fee and the net amount;
- **Wallets:** the payer and the payout address it went to;
- **Chain:** the block, plus a link to the transaction on Arcscan;
- **Links:** the payment session, subscription, customer or refund it belongs to.

Confirmed payments have a **Refund** button. It opens the refund dialog with this transaction already filled in. See [Refunds](/docs/dashboard/refunds).

## From the API

`GET /v1/transactions` takes the same filters, plus `sessionId` and `subscriptionId` to list the transactions of one payment session or one subscription.

To list a period, pass `from` and `to` as ISO-8601 timestamps with a time zone. `from` is included and `to` is not, so one month is:

```ts
await strimz.transactions.list({ from: '2026-10-01T00:00:00Z', to: '2026-11-01T00:00:00Z' })
```

The range applies to when the payment settled on Arc. See the [endpoint list](/docs/api-reference).
