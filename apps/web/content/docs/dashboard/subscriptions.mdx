A **subscription** pairs a [plan](/docs/dashboard/plans) (how much, how often) with a **payer wallet** (who pays). The permit the customer signs at checkout lets Strimz charge each period without asking again.

To create or archive plans, use the [Plans page](/docs/dashboard/plans).

## The layout

The table lists subscriptions, most recent first, with status filters and cards for each status. Columns:

- **Subscriber:** the payer wallet, which opens the subscription's page.
- **Plan:** the plan name, which opens the plan's page.
- **Amount**, **Status** and when the current period ends.

## A subscription's page

Each subscription shows:

- **Billing:** its plan, payer wallet and trial end, plus the current period and the next charge.
- **Charges:** every charge, each linking to its transaction. Refunds start from the transaction's page.
- **Cancel:** for active, trialing and at-risk subscriptions. Strimz marks it cancelled at once and cancels it on-chain. Cancelled subscriptions cannot be resumed; the customer subscribes again.

## Statuses

- **trialing:** in the free trial; the first charge comes when it ends.
- **active:** charged on schedule. Each successful charge fires `subscription.charged`.
- **at risk:** a charge failed, and the grace period is running. The AutoPay Agent's Recovery can email the customer; see [Agents](/docs/dashboard/agents).
- **paused:** not charging.
- **cancelled:** you or the customer cancelled. No more charges.
- **lapsed:** a charge failed and the grace period ended without a successful charge.

## Common workflows

**A customer wants to change plans.** Cancel their subscription and send them the new plan's sign-up link. The permit is per plan, so there is no in-place change.

**A charge failed.** Open the subscription and check its charges. If Recovery is on, it emails the customer during the grace period.
