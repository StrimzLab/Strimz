A **plan** is the shape of a recurring charge: how much, in which currency and how often. Customers subscribe to a plan through its sign-up link.

## The layout

The table lists your plans. Filter by **active**, **archived** or **all**. Each row shows the plan name, its price per interval, the free trial and its status. The name opens the plan's page.

## Creating a plan

Click **New plan** and fill in:

- **Plan name** (required): up to 120 characters.
- **Price** (required) and **Currency** (required): at least 1.00, in USDC or EURC.
- **Interval** (required): daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly.
- **Bill every** (optional): bill every 2 weeks, every 3 months and so on; 1 if left empty, up to 365.
- **Free trial** (optional): 0 to 365 days.
- **Description** (optional): up to 500 characters.
- **Success URL** and **Cancel URL** (optional): where the full-page checkout sends subscribers after they subscribe or cancel. `{SUBSCRIPTION_ID}` and `{ON_CHAIN_TX_HASH}` are filled in. See [Plans: return URLs](/docs/subscriptions/plans#return-urls).

The dashboard opens the new plan's page when it is created.

A plan's price, interval and trial can't be edited after creation. To change them, create a new plan and archive the old one. Existing subscribers stay on the plan they signed up for. The return URLs can be changed at any time.

## A plan's page

Each plan shows its price, billing interval, trial, description and ID, plus:

- **Sign-up link:** `/sub/<plan id>` on your Strimz domain, with a copy button. Send it to customers; it opens the hosted subscription checkout. Below it is a copyable `<StrimzSubscribeButton>` snippet for your own site.
- **After checkout:** the plan's success and cancel URLs, or "Not set". **Edit** changes them; leave a field blank to clear it.
- **Subscribers:** the subscriptions on this plan. Each opens the subscription's page.
- **Archive:** stops new sign-ups. Existing subscribers keep being charged until they cancel. Archiving cannot be undone.

## Storefront

Storefront products of type subscription pick one of your active plans. If you have none, the product dialog links here.
