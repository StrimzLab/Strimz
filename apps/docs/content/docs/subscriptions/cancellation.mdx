---
title: Cancellation
description: Cancelling is immediate. How you cancel from the API, how a subscriber cancels from Strimz's hosted page or their wallet, and how to tell who cancelled.
---

A cancel stops charging right away. There is no "cancel at period end" mode. A subscription can be cancelled by you, through the API or the dashboard, or by the subscriber, from Strimz's hosted page or straight from their wallet.

Every cancel sends exactly one `subscription.cancelled` webhook, and the subscription's `cancelledBy` says who cancelled it:

| `cancelledBy` | Who cancelled                                                    |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `payer`       | The subscriber, from the hosted page or their wallet             |
| `merchant`    | You, through the API or dashboard, or your merchant owner wallet |
| `strimz`      | Strimz, on chain                                                 |

A subscription that runs out of retries is not cancelled. It moves to `lapsed`, a separate status with its own webhook.

## Cancelling from the API

```ts
await strimz.subscriptions.cancel({
  id: 'cmq4wck6a0002je6lfmb5bgjp',
  reason: 'customer request',
})
```

`reason` is optional and stored on the subscription for your own audit.

What happens during the call:

1. The status becomes `cancelled`, `cancelledAt` is set, and `cancelledBy` is `merchant`.
2. `subscription.cancelled` fires with that subscription.
3. Strimz stops charging the subscription.

Cancelling a subscription that is already `cancelled` or `lapsed` returns an error.

The API also queues an on-chain cancel so the contract matches. When it lands, nothing else changes and no second webhook is sent.

## The subscriber's cancel page

Strimz hosts a page where subscribers see and cancel their subscriptions: `https://checkout.strimz.finance/manage`.

1. The subscriber connects the wallet they subscribed with.
2. They sign a message to prove the wallet is theirs. It costs nothing and moves no money.
3. They see that wallet's open subscriptions, across every merchant on Strimz.
4. **Cancel subscription** asks for one more signature. Strimz submits the cancel on chain and pays the network fee.
5. Once the cancel is confirmed and recorded, the page says "Cancelled. You won't be charged again."

Every receipt and reminder email Strimz sends a subscriber links to this page, with the subscription selected: `/manage?subscription=<id>`. You can link to it from your own product too.

The page never shows one wallet's subscriptions to another wallet.

## Cancelling from the payer's wallet

The contract's `cancel(uint256 subscriptionId)` can also be called by the payer directly, from any wallet tool that can send a contract call. Strimz sees the `SubscriptionCancelled` event on chain, marks the subscription `cancelled` with `cancelledBy: 'payer'`, and sends `subscription.cancelled`.

## Building a cancel button for your customers

Most products give customers a cancel button in their own app:

1. Build the page in your app.
2. Authenticate the customer the way you already do.
3. From your server, call `strimz.subscriptions.cancel({ id, reason })` with your secret key.

The secret key stays on your server. See [Self-serve cancel](/docs/recipes/self-serve-cancel) for a full example.

## Refunding unused time

Cancelling does not refund anything. If you want to give back the unused part of the current period, issue a refund against the last charge's transaction. There is no automatic proration, because the right amount depends on your pricing, discounts and tax rules.

A simple prorated amount:

```ts
const periodMs = Date.parse(sub.currentPeriodEndAt) - Date.parse(sub.currentPeriodStartAt)
const unusedMs = Math.max(0, Date.parse(sub.currentPeriodEndAt) - Date.now())
const refundAmount = (BigInt(sub.amount) * BigInt(unusedMs)) / BigInt(periodMs)
```

Then issue the refund from the dashboard's [Refunds page](/docs/dashboard/refunds), where your Strimz wallet signs the transfer. From code, `strimz.refunds.create` returns the refund and the transfer to sign; see [Process a refund](/docs/guides/refunds).

## Allowances

Cancelling does not revoke the payer's token allowance to the subscriptions contract. The contract refuses to charge a cancelled subscription, so the leftover allowance cannot be used for it. A payer who wants to remove it can call `approve(strimzSubscriptions, 0)` on the token from their wallet.
