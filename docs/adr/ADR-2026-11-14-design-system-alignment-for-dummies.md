# Strimz colours everywhere, and a quieter homepage hero (plain English)

## What's wrong

The designer's colour rulebook sets the exact greens, greys, border shades and the colours for success, warning and error. Over time the apps drifted from it:

- **Green text** uses a brighter green than the rulebook allows.
- **Status colours** were picked screen by screen.
- **Dark mode** uses a plain near-black instead of Strimz navy.

At the top of the homepage there were three things the founder wants gone:

- the moving particles behind the dashboard card;
- the green glow;
- the frame around the section.

## What we'll do

- Put the rulebook's colours into the one shared colour file, which every app reads from.
- Make dark mode use Strimz navy, with the exact shades approved by the founder.
- Remove those three things from the homepage hero, and nothing else.

## What changes for people

Every screen uses the same colours. The hero is calmer. Layouts, buttons, animations and everything else stay as they are.
