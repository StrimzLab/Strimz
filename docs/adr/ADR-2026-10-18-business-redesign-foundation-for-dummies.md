# ADR for Dummies: The Strimz homepage, made cinematic

- **Status:** Accepted (maintainer approved 2026-10-18)
- **Date:** 2026-10-18

## The Idea

We kept the Strimz homepage you know and rebuilt it to feel like a 2026 product launch.

- **What stays:** the green and navy, the dashboard in the hero, the coins, the triple green
  stripe and the logo tile.
- **What changes:** the words now speak to business owners, and every section has its own
  carefully made motion.

## What the Person Sees

1. **A stage at the top,** light in light mode and dark in dark mode. Thousands of tiny green lights stream in from the edges and pour
   into the live Strimz dashboard. The headline says "Stablecoin billing for…", and the last words
   melt between SaaS teams, marketplaces, creators, agencies and global B2B.
2. **The kinds of businesses** Strimz is for, drifting past in two rows.
3. **One big sentence** that comes into focus as you scroll: one link, one approval,
   every renewal charged on schedule, and the money in your own wallet in seconds.
4. **"How it works" stays on screen while you scroll.** A small dashboard acts out each step:
   - creating a plan;
   - sharing the link;
   - the customer approving in their wallet;
   - the money arriving.
5. **The three coins,** floating over falling green light, with the three promises: no double
   charges, subscriptions that don't expire, paid in seconds.
6. **Five panels that stack like cards** as you scroll: subscriptions, failed-payment recovery,
   refunds, invoices and reports. Each one shows its own little screen in action.
7. **For developers:** a 3D keyboard that types a real Strimz setup, copyable install commands and
   code samples.
8. **Prices,** and a calculator showing your first year's fees and what you keep.
9. **Questions and answers,** then a final invitation where light runs along circuit lines into
   the Strimz logo.
10. **A footer,** light or dark with your theme and set well apart from the final invitation, where the coins are drawn in green characters that light up under your mouse, with
    a giant "Strimz" rising into view.

## Important Limitation

- **No fake customers, logos or volume.** Example screens use sample data.
- **No comparisons with cards or other processors.** The page shows what Strimz does.
- **Example screens** describe one believable small business and say they are samples.
- **The page is long,** because the "How it works" section holds the screen for a while.
- **The pricing, about and contact pages** keep their current content for now, under the new menu
  and footer.

## What Changes

- A new homepage, menu and footer, in light and dark.
- **When something fails, the page says so:**
  - If copying a command fails, a message explains why and what to do.
  - If one section breaks, that section shows "Try again" and the rest keeps working.
  - Slow parts show grey placeholders while they load.
- **Our own pop-up messages** match the brand and work with screen readers.
- **People who turn motion off** see every section complete and still.

## What Does Not Change

- The colours, fonts and the light/dark switch.
- Prices, the dashboard, sign in, checkout and how payments work.
