# ADR for Dummies: About and Contact pages a business can trust

- **Status:** Accepted (maintainer approved 2026-10-19)
- **Date:** 2026-10-19

## The Idea

The About page talks about card companies instead of explaining why a business can trust Strimz with its money. The Contact page shows the same email address three times, and the site logs an error because of it. We'll rebuild both in the new design, using only claims we can back up.

## What the Person Sees

**About**

1. One line on what Strimz is for, and a sentence on what it does.
2. **Why stablecoins for business:**
   - you're paid in seconds;
   - a payment is final once it settles;
   - customers anywhere can pay without needing gas.
3. **How we keep your money safe:**
   - we never hold your money;
   - every payment has a receipt anyone can check;
   - our contracts have been through a private security audit.
4. A short band of true facts: Arc, two stablecoins, no gas for your customers, no funds held by us.
5. The same closing panel as the homepage.

**Contact**

1. "Talk to a real person."
2. One inbox, support@strimz.finance, with a guide to what to put in the subject for sales, support or security, and a **Book a demo** button that starts that email for you.
3. The same message form as now, next to priority-support and office-hours notes.

## Important Limitation

- **No comparisons with cards** or other payment companies.
- **No team section** at launch.
- **The audit claim is yours to stand behind.** There's no public report, so we only say it was a private audit.

## What Changes

- Both pages get the new look, with keycap buttons.
- The repeated-inbox error goes away.

## What Does Not Change

- The message form and where it sends.
- The reply times and office hours you confirmed.
