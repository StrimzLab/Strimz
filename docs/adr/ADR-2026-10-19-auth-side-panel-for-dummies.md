# ADR for Dummies: A sign-up panel that speaks to business owners

- **Status:** Accepted (maintainer approved 2026-10-19)
- **Date:** 2026-10-19

## The Idea

The dark panel beside the sign-in and sign-up forms talks like a developer tool: "infrastructure for the next billion businesses", "One API". We'll make it speak to the person signing up their business, and make it calmer when someone is just signing back in.

## What the Person Sees

**Signing up:**

- "Get paid in USDC and EURC, straight to your wallet."
- Three short benefits:
  - paid in seconds;
  - the money goes into your own wallet;
  - free on your first $1,000.
- A small "payment received" card, marked "Sample".

**Signing in:**

- "Welcome back," a line saying everything is where you left it, and a small "this month" card, also marked "Sample".

**On a phone:** the sign-up page shows the three benefits in a slim row above the form, so you still see the form straight away.

## Important Limitation

- **No "next billion"** or other claims we can't back up.
- **No comparisons with cards.** The issue's "no chargebacks" is swapped for "into your own wallet".
- **Example cards are labelled "Sample".**

## What Changes

- The words and picture in the side panel.
- The "Live on Arc" note at the bottom becomes "Settled in USDC and EURC", and it can no longer be cut off.

## What Does Not Change

- The sign-in and sign-up forms, and how signing in works.
